# lord-collection
Here you will find all types of ways to keep check on your lord collection (MR) on different platforms.

# <ins> How to change your lord icon from grey to colored </ins>

## <ins> YOU MUST HAVE ADOBE PHOTOSHOP ON YOUR LAPTOP / PC </ins> 

After you have downloaded the 'lord collection.psd'. follow the steps under this.

**1. Open the layers panel on the right side of your screen**


![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/layer-panel.png)

**2. There you should see a 'Black & white 1' contrast after scrolling down the layer panel**

![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/layer-contrast-hood-grey.png)

EVERYTHING under the contrast will de displayed as grey colored. to make them colored, you need to drag the icon name in the layer panel above the contrast and you should see this as result.
![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/layer-hood-colored.png)

**3. Edit the number text**

Press T on your keyboard or go to the left side of your screen and press on this icon. 
![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/layer-Text.png)

Afterwards click on the numbers displayed on the top of each row to edit the numbers to your liking.
![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/layer-Text-click.png)


**4. Export as PNG** 

If you have finished your lord collection, press V on your keyboard or click on this icon on the left side of your screen. Then press on 'file' top left on your screen.
![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/File.png)

click on Export ---> Quick epport as PNG

![Screenshot of a comment on a GitHub issue showing an image, added in the Markdown, of an Octocat smiling and raising a tentacle.](/file-export.png)


Congratulations on your lord collection :)
